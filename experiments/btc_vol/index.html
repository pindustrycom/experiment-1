BTC volatility study

Can modelling Bitcoin's volatility make money? Five rounds of tests (Kalman + GRU, Heston, rough volatility, real Deribit option prices), all walk-forward, all paper only.

Verdict: volatility forecasts pay through sizing, not direction. No model predicts which way BTC moves. Scaling the BTC breakout position by recent volatility does help, and it is now a live paper pot (Markets, Short-term, "BTC breakout, vol-sized"). Selling options when implied vol is above our forecast also earns money, but it is not yet proven on daily data. A daily Deribit option-chain collector started on 30 October 2026 to settle that.

The live pot: BTC breakout, sized by volatility

Same signal as the BTC breakout pot (4-hour Donchian channel). While long, the position is 2.5% / BTC's daily volatility over the past 30 days, up to 2x the pot when BTC is calm and less when it is wild, reset once a day only if the size moves 25% or more. 25 bp a side and the 6% daily stop, like the plain pot. Half-Kelly leverage instead (round 5): +36.0% a year from 2021 at the same Sharpe (0.90) and a deeper worst drop (-47%), so it adds leverage, not edge. Sizing with the sharper GRU forecast did worse than plain 30-day volatility.

The forecast's job is timing: it skips months when implied vol is too cheap, which were most of the crash months. Most of the gain came in 2021 and 2024, and 2025-26 earned only about 1-2% a year, so there is no option pot yet. The real daily-entry test runs once about three months of daily chains are collected.
