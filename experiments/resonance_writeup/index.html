Resonant sensors, with controls

Joaquin Philco, creator of the resonance architecture · October 2026 · 3-page PDF

What a month of resonance experiments showed. Every result is compared with controls that remove only the oscillation (low-pass, phase-scrambled, shuffled timing) and with simple learners on the same features.

  • Resonance works as a sensor front end. Spiking Heidelberg Digits: an untrained resonator bank plus a linear readout gets 85.7% vs 82.7% low-pass and 83.2% for the dataset paper's best network (state of the art 96.3%). Spoken words: 73.5% vs 72.8% log-mel and 36% low-pass. Eye movements: 92.6% vs 91.1% for the static image, and the best open-world novelty detection measured.
  • Learning rules after the front end did not beat simple controls. Resonance-trained lattices tie frozen random ones, pruning during sleep never helped, and memory cells score the same with real, low-pass or phase-scrambled resonators.
