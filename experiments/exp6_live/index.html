Type a word, draw a character or pick a colour. Every number on this page is computed from the image by the trained exp6 model: retina, Gabor V1, resonator body, letter structures, letter cells with soma and axon resonators, word structures and the naming associations.

Resonance index, resonance-based semantics and the rule set: Joaquin Philco.

What is live

Nothing here is scripted. The page loads the structures the model learned in training (…) and runs the same forward pass as the Python code, step by step. The fonts in your browser were never seen in training, so expect mistakes: on unseen fonts the model reads characters right about half the time and words about 86% of the time. It only knows 11 words; any other word is answered with the closest one.

What runs, in order

  1. Retina. The 28 × 28 cell is split into luminance and red, green, blue and yellow ON cells.
  2. V1. Each channel is filtered by complex Gabor cells at 4 orientations and 2 scales.
  3. Resonator body. Each channel drives its own bank of 32 damped resonators along the scan line, then 3 shared banks of 64. Energy in 4 bands per resonator gives 1,408 nodes.
  4. Letter structures. 36 structures learned by co-resonance above background each resonate on the nodes (aᵀWa / ‖a‖²).
  5. Letter cells. The eye holds each cell for 3 steps, then a 1-step saccade. The positive z-scored resonance is the input current of 36 letter cells; each soma integrates it with a leak and its axon drives resonators at 4 frequencies. Energy and end-of-word phase (ON/OFF) give 576 word nodes.
  6. Word structures (11) and word sets (3) resonate on the word nodes. Colour patches go through steps 1 to 3 and the associations resonate on them.

Model: alphabet/words_world.py --phase, exported by alphabet/export_live.py. Training fonts: DejaVu, FreeSans, FreeSerif, Liberation Sans and Mono.
